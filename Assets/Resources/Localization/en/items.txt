# English item names, descriptions and lore.
黄铜钥匙	Brass Key
能打开储藏室抽屉的小钥匙。	A small key that opens the storage room drawer.
女仆长每晚都用它锁好银器。最后一页值夜记录只写了半句：「抽屉里多了别人的影子。」	The head maid used it every night to lock the silver. The last page of the night log reads only: "There is someone else's shadow in the drawer."
保险丝	Fuse
配电间电闸缺失的零件。	A missing part for the breaker switch in the utility room.
停电那晚，维修工在总闸下捡到一堆烧黑的保险丝，只有这一枚是完好的。	The night the power failed, the repairman found a pile of burnt fuses under the main breaker. Only this one was intact.
日之徽记	Sun Emblem
封印大门三枚徽记之一，呈琥珀色。	One of the three emblems for the sealed gate. Amber colored.
庄园的门要三枚徽记同时归位才能打开。日之徽记一直挂在餐厅壁炉上方，谁也不许碰。	The manor gate opens only when three emblems are placed together. The Sun Emblem hung above the dining room fireplace, and no one was allowed to touch it.
月之徽记	Moon Emblem
封印大门三枚徽记之一，呈月蓝色。	One of the three emblems for the sealed gate. Moon-blue.
旧照片里，庄园主的女儿把它缝在礼服内衬，说这样夜里就不会做噩梦。	In an old photograph, the manor lord's daughter sewed it into her dress lining, saying it kept the nightmares away.
星之徽记	Star Emblem
封印大门三枚徽记之一，呈星紫色。	One of the three emblems for the sealed gate. Star-purple.
第三枚徽记在八音盒的暗格里躺了几十年，直到有人重新拧紧了发条。	The third emblem lay in a music box's hidden compartment for decades, until someone wound the spring again.
陈年红酒	Aged Red Wine
餐厅长桌上留下的收藏品，已经落满灰尘。	A collector's bottle left on the dining table, now covered in dust.
瓶塞上印着 1937。那场告别宴会只开了一半，客人再也没有回来取走剩下的酒。	The cork is stamped 1937. The farewell banquet ended halfway through, and the guests never returned for the remaining wine.
银茶杯	Silver Teacup
会客厅茶桌上的银质茶杯，杯沿刻着庄园纹章。	A silver teacup on the parlor table, its rim engraved with the manor crest.
杯底残留着一圈褐色茶渍，像有人喝到一半忽然被叫走，再没回来。	A ring of brown tea stain remains at the bottom, as if someone was called away mid-drink and never returned.
八音盒	Music Box
音乐室钢琴上的八音盒，仍能奏出一段残缺的旋律。	A music box on the music room piano. It can still play a broken melody.
曲子总在同一个音上停住——庄园主的女儿失踪那天，发条正好卡在这里。	The tune always stops on the same note. On the day the lord's daughter vanished, the spring jammed right there.
旧照片	Old Photograph
客房里一张泛黄的合影，记录着庄园昔日的住客。	A yellowed group photo in the guest room, showing the manor's former residents.
照片背后写着十一个人的名字，可画面里站了十二个人。多出来的那个没有脸。	Eleven names are written on the back, but twelve people stand in the picture. The extra one has no face.
地下室钥匙	Basement Key
一把锈迹斑斑的旧钥匙，能打开锅炉房的地窖铁门。	A rusty old key that opens the cellar door in the boiler room.
锅炉工常年把它挂在脖子上，最后却被人发现锁在地窖门的内侧。	The stoker always wore it around his neck, but it was eventually found locked on the inside of the cellar door.
手电筒	Flashlight
按 E 开关的旧式手电筒，是探索黑暗地下室的关键。	An old flashlight toggled with E. Essential for exploring the dark basement.
守夜人的手电。灯罩上缠着一圈歪斜的胶布，像是有人在黑暗里匆忙缠上去的。	The night watchman's light. Tape is wrapped crookedly around the lens, as if someone did it hastily in the dark.
电梯齿轮	Elevator Gear
电梯传动箱缺失的铜制齿轮，沉甸甸的。	The heavy brass gear missing from the elevator gearbox.
检修工在日志里写：「齿轮换好之前，别让任何人独自乘电梯上四层。」	A repairman wrote in the log: "Until the gear is replaced, never let anyone ride the elevator to the fourth floor alone."
影之徽记	Shadow Emblem
封印大门的第四枚徽记，呈暗紫色，藏在庄园地下。	The fourth emblem of the sealed gate, dark purple and hidden beneath the manor.
三枚徽记封的是门，第四枚封的是门后的东西。传说它被埋在地窖最深处。	Three emblems seal the door; the fourth seals what lies behind it. It is said to be buried at the deepest point of the cellar.
银烛台	Silver Candlestick
地下储藏室里翻出的银烛台，烛泪已经凝成琥珀色。	A silver candlestick found in the underground storage room, its wax hardened to amber.
烛泪里嵌着半枚指纹，比烛台本身更让人不安。	Half a fingerprint is embedded in the wax, more unsettling than the candlestick itself.
黄铜怀表	Brass Pocket Watch
表针早已停摆，表壳内侧刻着庄园的旧纹章。	The hands stopped long ago. The old manor crest is engraved inside the case.
三枚指针都停在 3:17——正是疗养院全楼断电的那一分钟。	All three hands stopped at 3:17, the very minute the sanatorium lost power.
黑胶唱片	Vinyl Record
一张边缘破损的黑胶唱片，标签上写着某场旧宴会。	A damaged vinyl record with an old banquet written on its label.
标签上的宴会日期被人用刀片刮掉了，只剩半行乐队签名。	The banquet date was scraped off with a blade, leaving only half a band signature.
旧日记	Old Diary
纸张发脆的日记，记录着庄园仆人在地下走廊里的见闻。	A diary with brittle pages, recording what the manor servants saw in the underground corridors.
「墙里有人在走。」这句话被反复写了十几遍，字迹一次比一次用力。	"Someone is walking inside the walls." The sentence is written a dozen times, each more forceful than the last.
青花瓷瓶	Blue-and-White Vase
缺了一角的青花瓷瓶，仍然看得出昔日的光泽。	A blue-and-white vase with a chipped corner, still showing its former luster.
缺口很新。瓶身内部的裂纹里，卡着一小片深色的布。	The chip is recent. A small piece of dark cloth is caught in a crack inside the vase.
黄铜罗盘	Brass Compass
指针不断晃动，似乎对地下的某些地方格外敏感。	The needle keeps trembling, as if sensitive to something underground.
无论怎么转，指针最后都会垂向地下走廊的方向，像被什么牵着。	No matter how you turn it, the needle always settles toward the underground corridor, as if pulled by something.
旧硬币	Old Coin
面值早已无法辨认的银币，边缘磨得发亮。	A silver coin whose value is no longer legible, its rim worn bright.
疗养院食堂的代币，背面刻着「凭此换取一份热汤」。	A sanatorium canteen token. The back reads: "Exchange this for one hot soup."
半截蜡烛	Half Candle
选中后可照亮身边 5 米，最多燃烧 60 秒；切换走会暂停燃烧。	When selected, lights 5 meters around you for up to 60 seconds. Switching away pauses the burn.
停电的夜里，病房里的人靠这样的半截蜡烛熬到天亮。有些人没熬过去。	On blackout nights, patients survived until dawn by candles like this. Some did not.
碎布片	Cloth Scrap
从旧窗帘上撕下来的布片，沾着灰尘。	A scrap torn from an old curtain, covered in dust.
边缘是整齐的撕口，更像是有人刻意扯下来当作标记。	The torn edge is unusually neat, as if someone ripped it off deliberately as a marker.
空酒瓶	Empty Bottle
瓶底还残留着一点酒渍的旧玻璃瓶。	An old glass bottle with a little wine stain left at the bottom.
车库守夜人靠它打发长夜，瓶身贴着一张手写便签：「别往西边走。」	The garage night watchman passed the long nights with it. A handwritten note on the bottle reads: "Don't go west."
旧电池	Old Battery
外壳有些鼓包的旧电池，早就不通电了。	An old battery with a bulging casing, long dead.
手电筒的备用电池，被谁一枚枚换下来，堆在抽屉最里侧。	Spare batteries for the flashlight, swapped out one by one and piled at the back of a drawer.
绷带	Bandage
卷得整整齐齐的旧绷带，边缘已经发黄。	An old bandage rolled neatly, its edges yellowed.
绷带上没有血迹，却有一股连消毒水都盖不住的霉味。	There is no blood on the bandage, but a musty smell that even disinfectant cannot hide.
药瓶	Pill Bottle
标签被撕掉一半的药瓶，里面只剩几粒白色药片。	A pill bottle with half its label torn off, holding only a few white tablets.
撕掉的那半张标签上还能认出两个字：「镇静」。	Two characters are still visible on the torn label: "Sedative."
旧报纸	Old Newspaper
日期已经模糊的旧报纸，头版是一则失踪启事。	An old newspaper with a blurred date. The front page is a missing-person notice.
启事里失踪的七个人，照片与庄园那张合影上的面孔一模一样。	The seven people reported missing have the same faces as those in the manor group photo.
火柴盒	Matchbox
还剩几根火柴的旧纸盒，盒面印着模糊的旅店标志。	An old paper matchbox with a few matches left, its hotel logo faded.
盒面的旅店标志和疗养院门厅的徽章如出一辙，像出自同一个人之手。	The hotel logo on the box is identical to the crest in the sanatorium lobby, as if drawn by the same hand.
肥皂	Soap
用得只剩薄片的一块肥皂，还带着淡淡的味道。	A soap worn down to a thin slice, still faintly scented.
洗衣房的肥皂总在半夜少一块。值夜的人从不敢去问是谁拿的。	The laundry soap always loses a piece at midnight. The night staff never dared ask who took it.
二层通行卡	2F Access Card
印着「2F 病区」的磁卡，可打开二层病区门。	A card marked "2F Ward" that opens the second-floor ward door.
背面印着护士的工号，边缘被反复刷卡磨得发白。	A nurse's ID number is printed on the back, and the edge is worn white from repeated swiping.
三层通行卡	3F Access Card
印着「3F 手术区」的磁卡，可打开三层手术区门。	A card marked "3F Surgery" that opens the third-floor surgery door.
卡面下方有一道擦不掉的暗色指印，正好落在照片里手术室门的位置。	A dark fingerprint that cannot be wiped off sits right where the operating room door appears in the photo.
四层通行卡	4F Access Card
印着「4F 行政区」的磁卡，可打开四层行政区门。	A card marked "4F Administration" that opens the fourth-floor administration door.
裂缝从持卡人照片的颈部横穿过去，像有人故意折断过一次。	A crack runs across the cardholder's photo at the neck, as if someone deliberately snapped it once.
药库钥匙	Pharmacy Key
药房柜子的钥匙，钥匙柄上缠着褪色的胶布。	The pharmacy cabinet key, its handle wrapped in faded tape.
胶布上用铅笔写着「只借一次」，字迹被人反复描粗。	"Borrow once only" is penciled on the tape, traced over again and again.
手术区钥匙	Surgery Key
手术室器械柜的钥匙，冰冷得不太正常。	The key to the operating room instrument cabinet. It feels unnaturally cold.
握久了指尖会发麻，比这层楼里任何金属都冷。	Hold it long enough and your fingertips go numb. It is colder than any metal on this floor.
值班钥匙	Duty Key
四层值班室的门钥匙，钥匙环上还挂着交接班牌。	The fourth-floor duty room key, with a shift handover tag still on the ring.
交接班牌上最后一个名字被人用力划掉了，划痕深得几乎穿透铁皮。	The last name on the tag was scratched out hard, deep enough to almost pierce the metal.
院长室钥匙	Director's Key
刻着「行政」二字的旧钥匙，齿口磨损严重。	An old key engraved with "Administration", its teeth badly worn.
齿口磨损得厉害，说明它被开过太多次——远超过一扇办公室的门所需。	The worn teeth show it was used far more often than one office door would require.
主门钥匙	Main Gate Key
挂在院长室铁环上的长柄钥匙，能打开一层正门。	A long-handled key on the iron ring in the director's office. It opens the ground-floor main gate.
逃生最近的钥匙，却被留在离正门最远的一间屋子里。	The key closest to escape was left in the room farthest from the main gate.
病房钥匙	Ward Key
一层病区通用的老钥匙，钥匙柄上缠着白色胶布。	An old master key for the ground-floor ward, its handle wrapped in white tape.
胶布上写着「1F 全线」。胶布底下，还压着另一行更旧的字。	"1F ALL" is written on the tape. Under it is another, older line of writing.
病人物品柜钥匙	Patient Locker Key
刻着床位号的小钥匙，能打开四层病房的储物柜。	A small key engraved with a bed number. It opens the fourth-floor patient lockers.
每把这样的钥匙对应一个床位，而四层的床位登记簿缺了整整一页。	Each key matches one bed, and the fourth-floor bed register is missing an entire page.
职员钥匙	Staff Key
地下值班室抽屉里的钥匙，能打开地下档案室。	A key from the underground duty room drawer. It opens the underground archive.
档案室的钥匙本该有三把，行政记录里却只登记了一把。	There should be three archive keys, but the administration records list only one.
库房钥匙	Storehouse Key
洗衣房抽屉里的钥匙，齿口很浅，只能开地下库房。	A key from the laundry drawer. Its teeth are shallow and it only opens the underground storehouse.
齿口很浅，只能开库房。而库房里堆着的东西，账本上一件都没写。	The shallow teeth only fit the storehouse, and none of the things piled inside appear in the ledger.
保险丝 · 甲	Fuse A
总闸缺的第一枚保险丝，铜帽还亮着。	The first fuse missing from the main breaker, its brass cap still bright.
铜帽还亮着，像是刚被人从别的配电箱里拆下来。	The cap is so bright it looks as if someone just pulled it from another breaker box.
保险丝 · 乙	Fuse B
总闸缺的第二枚保险丝，玻璃管里有一道裂纹。	The second fuse missing from the main breaker, with a crack in its glass tube.
玻璃管里有一道裂纹，却干净得不沾一丝灰——它从没被装上过。	There is a crack in the glass, yet it is spotless. It was never installed.
保险丝 · 丙	Fuse C
总闸缺的第三枚保险丝，用油纸包着。	The third fuse missing from the main breaker, wrapped in oil paper.
油纸包得很仔细，像有人打算把它留到「那一天」再用。	The oil paper is wrapped carefully, as if someone meant to save it for "that day."
车库钥匙	Garage Key
挂着木牌的粗钥匙，能打开负一层车库门。	A thick key with a wooden tag. It opens the basement garage door.
木牌上「负一层·车库」被人添了一笔，改成了「负一层·别进去」。	Someone added a stroke to the tag, changing "B1 Garage" to "B1 Do Not Enter."
卷帘门曲柄	Shutter Crank
一根 L 形手摇曲柄，用来摇起车库卷帘门。	An L-shaped hand crank used to raise the garage shutter.
曲柄上缠着机油和布条，说明最后一次使用非常匆忙。	Oil and cloth strips are wrapped around the crank, showing the last use was very rushed.
消防连廊钥匙	Fire Corridor Key
红色钥匙牌上写着「4F 东侧消防连廊」。	A red key tag reads "4F East Fire Corridor."
钥匙牌背面贴着消防演习的日期——同一天，疗养院被永久关闭。	The back of the tag bears a fire drill date, the same day the sanatorium was permanently closed.
消防斧	Fire Axe
斧刃有些卷口，但仍能撬开锈死的盖板。	The blade is nicked but can still pry open a rusted cover.
斧柄上刻着「最后一次检查：3:17」，和那块怀表停在同一分钟。	The handle is carved with "Last inspection: 3:17", the same minute the pocket watch stopped.
